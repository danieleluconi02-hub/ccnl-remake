Become part of a dynamic research group at the forefront of computational cognitive neuroscience. We believe in fostering an inclusive and rigorous research environment where curiosity drives discovery.

Open Positions

We are recruiting

The CCNL at the University of Padova is actively recruiting talented and motivated researchers at all career levels. We regularly seek postdoctoral researchers, PhD students, and visiting scholars who share our passion for understanding the computational mechanisms underlying human cognition.

Whether your background is in AI, behavioural experiments, neuroimaging, or a combination of approaches, we have opportunities that match your interests and expertise.

01.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Experienced researchers leading independent projects and mentoring students

02.

PhD Students

Graduate students pursuing doctoral research in cognitive neuroscience

03.

Visiting Scholars

Researchers from other institutions seeking short or long-term collaborations

Research Areas We Recruit For

Area 01

Computational Modeling & AI

Deep neural networks, generative models, ML applications in cognitive science

Area 02

Numerical Cognition

How the brain processes numbers, magnitude, and mathematical concepts

Area 03

Reading & Dyslexia

Cognitive and neural mechanisms of reading acquisition and disorders

Area 04

Visuospatial Attention

Spatial cognition, visual attention, and awareness in the human brain

Area 05

Neuroimaging Methods

fMRI, EEG, fNIRS, and non-invasive brain stimulation

Area 06

Clinical Applications

Translating research into clinical tools for learning and neurological disorders
