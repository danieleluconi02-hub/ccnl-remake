Opportunities

Join the Lab

Become part of a dynamic research group at the forefront of computational cognitive neuroscience. We believe in fostering an inclusive and rigorous research environment where curiosity drives discovery.

Open Positions

We are recruiting

The CCNL at the University of Padova is actively recruiting talented and motivated researchers at all career levels. We regularly seek postdoctoral researchers, PhD students, and visiting scholars who share our passion for understanding the computational mechanisms underlying human cognition.

Whether your background is in AI, behavioral experiments, neuroimaging, or a combination of approaches, we have opportunities that match your interests and expertise.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Experienced researchers leading independent projects and mentoring students

02.

PhD Students

Graduate students pursuing doctoral research in cognitive neuroscience

03.

Visiting Scholars

Researchers from other institutions seeking short or long-term collaborations

Research Areas We Recruit For

Area 01

Artificial Intelligence & Foundation Models

Emergent cognitive abilities in large-scale multimodal AI models

Area 02

Numerical Cognition & Dyscalculia

How the brain encodes, processes, and manipulates numerical information

Area 03

Visuospatial Attention & Awareness

Neural and computational mechanisms of visuospatial attention

Area 04

Language Processing & Dyslexia

Connectionist and deep learning models of reading aloud

Area 05

Generative Deep Learning

Biologically-inspired generative models as theories of perception

Area 06

Clinical Neuroscience & Rehabilitation

Translating computational models into clinical tools for patients

Area 07

Connectionist Dual-Process Model

The CDP+ model of reading aloud: dual-route connectionist architecture
