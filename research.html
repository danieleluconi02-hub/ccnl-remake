Research Programme

Research
Areas & Methods

Our lab focuses on modelling human perception and cognition through biologically-inspired generative models and deep neural networks—a foundation established during the pioneering ERC GENMOD project. This computational framework integrates with empirical research using psychophysical and neuroimaging methods.

Index of Research Areas — Vol. 2000–2026

01.

Artificial Intelligence & Foundation Models

Emergent cognitive abilities in large-scale multimodal AI models. Comparative analysis of representational structures in neural networks and biological cognition.

deep learning · llms · cognitive emergence · neural representations
02.

Numerical Cognition & Dyscalculia

How the brain encodes, processes, and manipulates numerical information—from infant number sense to adult skilled arithmetic. Computational and empirical investigations of developmental dyscalculia.

number sense · magnitude · dyscalculia · development · cariparo grant
03.

Visuospatial Attention & Awareness

Neural and computational mechanisms of visuospatial attention. Studies of spatial neglect, hemispatial biases, and top-down attentional control using neuroimaging and brain stimulation.

attention · neglect · fmri · tms · spatial cognition
04.

Language Processing & Dyslexia

Connectionist and deep learning models of reading aloud (CDP+). Attentional basis of developmental dyslexia. Eye-tracking and neuroimaging studies of normal and impaired reading.

cdp model · reading · dyslexia · eye-tracking · phonology
05.

Generative Deep Learning

Biologically-inspired generative models (DBNs, VAEs, diffusion models) as theories of perception and cognition. ERC-funded GENMOD programme—self-learning AI that emulates the human brain.

erc genmod · dbn · vae · unsupervised learning · perception
06.

Clinical Neuroscience & Rehabilitation

Translating computational models into clinical tools for patients with neurological and neurodevelopmental disorders. Work at San Camillo Research Hospital (Venice-Lido).

stroke · rehabilitation · irccs san camillo · neuroplasticity
07.

Connectionist Dual-Process Model

The CDP+ model of reading aloud: dual-route connectionist architecture simulating both sublexical (phonological) and lexical processing pathways in skilled and beginning readers.

cdp+ · dual route · phonology · lexical · reading aloud

Research Programme

Computation as a Theory of Mind

The lab treats computational modelling not as a tool, but as a scientific language for expressing theories of cognition. Every model makes quantitative, falsifiable predictions.

This computational framework integrates with empirical research—psychophysics, eye-tracking, fMRI, and EEG—to triangulate on mechanisms across levels of analysis.
